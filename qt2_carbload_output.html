Carbohydrate Loading Protocol

How to use your carbohydrate loading plan
1
Follow the days in order. Start with two-days-before if included, then day before, then race morning. Each section is timed to top off your glycogen stores before the gun.
2
Hit your totals, not just the foods. The carbohydrate number at the bottom of each meal is what matters. Use the "or" alternatives if you can't find an exact food — they're pre-calculated to match.
3
Drink your sports drink. It's doing real work. Every meal includes a sports drink target. Sip it with your meal rather than all at once to keep your stomach happy.
4
Keep fat and fiber low. This is not the time for salads, fried food, or heavy sauces. Low fiber, low fat, familiar foods only — your gut will thank you on race day.
5
Race morning has two windows. Main meal 3 hours before start. Top-up 60 minutes out. Don't skip the top-up — it's the last chance to put fuel in the tank.
6
You may gain 1–3 lbs. That's water binding to glycogen — it's a good sign. It will be gone by mile 3.
